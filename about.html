About SchengenEase

SchengenEase is an independent travel and visa consultancy helping Indian travellers prepare Schengen short-stay applications and plan memorable trips across Europe.

We combine document support, itinerary design, and booking guidance so you can focus on your journey — not paperwork alone.

Important: We are not a government portal. Visa decisions are made only by embassies and consulates. Our fee covers consultancy services only.
