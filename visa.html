Schengen visa assistance

Short-stay visa (Type C) — up to 90 days in 180 days across 27 Schengen states

One visa, many countries

Apply through the consulate of your main destination (longest stay or first entry). Typical embassy fee: €80 for adults (paid directly to the mission — not included in our ₹2,999 file assistance).

Processing often takes 15 calendar days; peak season can be 30–60 days. Apply at least 3 weeks before travel, ideally 2–3 months ahead.
